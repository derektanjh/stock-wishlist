How the trend analysis works
Trendline & channelA best-fit line through the closing prices over the chosen period (fitted on a log scale, so it measures % growth). The shaded channel is ±2 standard deviations around it; about 95% of days fall inside.
Trend per yearHow fast the trendline is rising or falling, as a yearly %. Strength (R²) says how well prices follow the line: strong ≥ 0.6, steady ≥ 0.3, below that the moves are choppy.
vs trendlineHow far today's price is from the line, in standard deviations (σ). −1σ or lower in an uptrend is a dip; +1.5σ or higher is stretched.
50 / 200-day averageAverage close over the last 50 and 200 trading days. Price above the 200-day is a long-term uptrend; 50-day above the 200-day is a "golden cross", below is a "death cross".
RSI (14)Momentum over the last 14 days, 0–100. Under 30 is oversold, over 70 is overbought.
SignalBuy zone at or under your target, or a dip in an uptrend · Near target within 5% · Stretched well above trend or overbought · Downtrend · Watch everything else.
